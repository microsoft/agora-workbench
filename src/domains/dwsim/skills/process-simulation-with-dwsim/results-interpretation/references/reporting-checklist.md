# Reporting Checklist

Use this checklist when presenting simulation results to ensure completeness.

## For Every Simulation

- [ ] Convergence status (converged / errors)
- [ ] Overall mass balance: mass in, mass out, relative difference
- [ ] Overall energy balance: energy in, energy out, relative difference

## For Each Feed Stream

- [ ] Stream name
- [ ] Temperature (K) and pressure (Pa)
- [ ] Total molar flow (mol/s) and total mass flow (kg/s)
- [ ] Composition (mole fractions of key compounds)
- [ ] Phase (liquid, vapor, or two-phase)

## For Each Product Stream

- [ ] Stream name
- [ ] Temperature and pressure
- [ ] Total flow
- [ ] Composition with purity of target compound
- [ ] Vapor fraction

## For Heaters / Coolers

- [ ] Unit name
- [ ] Inlet and outlet temperatures
- [ ] Heat duty (W) — positive for heating, negative for cooling
- [ ] Pressure drop

## For Heat Exchangers

- [ ] Unit name
- [ ] Hot-side inlet and outlet temperatures
- [ ] Cold-side inlet and outlet temperatures
- [ ] Duty transferred (W)

## For Pumps / Compressors

- [ ] Unit name
- [ ] Inlet and outlet pressures
- [ ] Power duty (W)
- [ ] Efficiency

## For Reactors

- [ ] Unit name and type (conversion / equilibrium)
- [ ] Reaction conversion or equilibrium extent
- [ ] Heat duty (W) — exothermic (negative) or endothermic (positive)
- [ ] Product distribution (vapor vs. liquid outlet compositions)

## For Distillation Columns

- [ ] Unit name
- [ ] Number of stages, feed stage
- [ ] Reflux ratio
- [ ] Condenser duty (W) and reboiler duty (W)
- [ ] Distillate composition and purity
- [ ] Bottoms composition and purity

## For Flash Drums / Separators

- [ ] Unit name
- [ ] Operating temperature and pressure
- [ ] Vapor outlet flow and composition
- [ ] Liquid outlet flow and composition
- [ ] Split fraction (what fraction reports to vapor)
